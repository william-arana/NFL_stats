How to use this

1Browse two ways

By dataset shows what each load_ function returns. By stat category groups the same stat across sources, so you can see pressure from PFR, Next Gen and FTN side by side.

2Circle what matters

Gold Essential tags are my picks for waiver work. Pick anything else your gut says matters. Picks are saved in this browser.

3Hand it off

Open in Lab turns your picks into a query on live data you can sort, chart and download. Ask Claude about these loads them into the Lab's Ask box so you can ask for filters on exactly those stats. Or open My picks and copy the list.
